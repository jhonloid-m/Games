GAME GUIDE

📖 How to Play

🎮 How to play

• Drag a piece onto the 8×8 board.
• Fill a complete row or column to clear it.
• Use your 3 pieces, then a new set appears.
• Plan ahead. When none of your pieces can fit, the run ends unless you have a ❤️ Life.

📜 Rules

• Pieces cannot overlap or go outside the board.
• ❤️ Lives start at 0 every game and can be bought in the shop.
• You can buy at most 3 Hearts per game.
• 🪙 Each cleared line gives 2 coins, multiplied by your current combo. • 🧲 Coin Magnet doubles line-clear coins for 60 seconds.
• 🪙 Coins reset when you lose and are earned again each run.
• Leaderboard scores use your chosen nickname.

🛒 Shop limits

❤️ Heart: max 3/game
⚡ Score Boost: next 3 placements
🧲 Coin Magnet: 60 seconds
💎 Lucky Clear: next 1 line clear

🏆 Scoring

Clear multiple lines together for bigger bonuses. Keep your combo alive to raise the multiplier up to ×8. Fever Mode starts at ×4 combo.
